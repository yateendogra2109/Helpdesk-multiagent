Source: IIT Jodhpur Academic Regulations (UG), Section 11, Table 11.2 (transcribed from a screenshot of the regulations; see also Academic_Regulations_2019_onwards.pdf, page 17).

## Additional learning credits and Dual Degree specialization (text just before Section 11.3)

Starting from the sixth semester, students having earned 80 credits with CGPA more than or equal to 6.0 are permitted to take up to 4 extra credits as part of their normal load, which forms part of her/his additional learning component, every semester in addition to the prescribed courses for their degree.

(b) B.Tech. + M.Tech. Dual Degree in the chosen specialization: a student needs to complete a total of 56 credits in the Minor, Department, or Interdisciplinary area offered. It will include credits earned for Major/Department or Minor/Interdisciplinary area Specialization excluding the project credits.

## Open Elective credits required for a B.Tech. degree

A B.Tech. Regular student must earn 10 credits of Open Electives (OE). A B.Tech. student with Specialization earns 0 Open Elective credits (the 12 credits of Specialization Electives replace them), and so does a Dual Degree student.

## Total credits required for a B.Tech. degree

B.Tech. Regular: 150 graded credits, or 165 credits including non-graded courses. B.Tech. with Specialization: 160 graded, 175 including non-graded. Dual Degree: 196 graded, 211 including non-graded.

## 11.4 Maximum Duration of the Program

The maximum duration for the completion of B.Tech. Programs shall be 14 registered semesters.
