## Notes
- L slots: for labs and tutorial for courses in F-J. M slots: for labs and tutorial for courses in A-E.
- While allocating slots for UG/PG compulsory courses, priority is given to students of graduating B.Tech with backlog 2 or less.
- Maximum students per tutorial: about 40. Tutorial slots are flexible (any timing) as per the convenience of students, instructors and classroom availability. Some slots are provided in the time table, but any other timing can also be considered based on classroom availability.
- Maximum students per lab: 50.
- All fractals of a course run in the same slot.
- To facilitate electives/overload for 2nd and 3rd year students, all Departments open at least 1 core/elective course in the K or N slot.
- Upper limit of HSS courses is 80, all elective courses is 80, and Management Minor course is 40.
